# The End is N-AI-gh: Amodei is Coming, Look Busy

A light-hearted, interactive slide-deck presentation for a UK Clinical Scientific Computing team on why we should stop hand-crafting boilerplate and reading 40,000-line pull requests line-by-line, and start investing in **high-leverage domain context** and **adversarial automated test harnesses**.

## Live Presentation

- **Keyboard Navigation:** `←` / `→` or `Space` to change slides
- **Speaker Notes:** Press `N` (or click **Notes** in the bottom bar)
- **Full-Screen Figures:** Click any benchmark plot, reconstruction gallery, or screenshot to zoom
- **Interactive Features:**
  - **Slide 2 (Dino Tycoon):** Switch between *Wild Acres Zoo (Era 1)*, *Dino Tycoon (Era 2)*, *Monorail, Sea & Sky (Era 3)*, and *Multi-Agent Swarm Testing* on the live automated park simulation canvas, launch the live game, or drag-and-drop local `.mp4` gameplay videos right onto the slide.
  - **Slide 3 (AutoSeg vs MELD Find & nnU-Net v2):** Switch across 50-epoch 3-fold benchmark curves and toggle `reproduce_upstream_bbox_bug` (`True` vs `False`) to see the upstream `nnUNetDataLoader.get_bbox` boundary bug uncovered during parity verification.
  - **Slide 4 (PhD Unified in 48 Hours):** Explore dual-track PET/SPECT reconstructions (`2Hop-Tsai-dTNV`), anatomical hallucination maps, and `FISTA-DP` solver convergence powered by 4 years of curated domain context.
  - **Slide 6 (Case Study — Geoff Burnhill's STRS App):** Explore how adversarial AI probes uncovered 23 defects (`B00`–`B22`) in a >40,000-line Class I regulated C# codebase that 396 passing unit tests and human code review missed.
  - **Slide 9 (Rapture Readiness Audit):** Interactive team checklist to determine whether you will be saved or left behind.
